22 selected demonstrations, from whole tissue sections to ultrastructure. Select a label to inspect the actual prediction, prompt and source.

Curated demonstrations, not an accuracy benchmark. These results were selected after prompt comparisons and visual inspection. Empty, invalid and unconvincing candidates are excluded from this gallery and documented in the research report. EM images are native 2D sections, never Z projections. Cards show timepoint 0; label downloads retain every computed timepoint.
